Frequently asked questions

I unfolded and nothing printed on the other half
  • On the folded screen, rub the spots you want to print yourself. Only the spots you rub are printed.
  • Paint added after a press is not printed by that press. Fold and press again.
  • Every fold starts with a clean back. Whatever was printed stays on the open paper.
I can't see my drawing when folded
  • The folded outer display is the back of the paper, so the drawing is not shown. You can only press.
  • Unfold and your drawing is right there on the inner display.
The iMessage sticker doesn't show up in Messages
  • Open the sheet, choose Export, and tap Add next to "iMessage sticker".
  • In a Messages conversation, open the app drawer and pick IOOI.
Saving to Photos doesn't work
  • The first time you save, IOOI asks to add photos. If you declined, check IOOI's Photos permission in the Settings app.
  • IOOI never reads your photos. It only adds your finished artwork.
Does it work on an iPhone that isn't a Duo?
  • IOOI is made for the folding iPhone Duo.
  • On an iPhone that doesn't fold you only get the pressing screen. Drawing needs a device with an inner display.
Where are my sheets saved?
  • Only on your device. IOOI doesn't send them to a server or back them up.
  • Deleting the app deletes your sheets too. Export the ones you want to keep to Photos or as a PDF.
