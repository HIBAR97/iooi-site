Decalcomania for iPhone Duo

Open up to a mirror image.

Paint on one half of the open paper, fold it and press the back. Unfold, and the spots you pressed slowly appear on the other half, mirrored.

iPhone Duo · iOS 27.1 or later

How it plays

Three moves. Folding and unfolding is the game.

  1. 1 {{@icon name="draw"}}

    Paint

    Draw on either half of the open paper with a finger or Apple Pencil. Pick the color and brush size from the side buttons.

  2. 2 {{@icon name="fold"}}

    Fold and press

    Fold your iPhone Duo and the outer display becomes the back of the paper. Rub the spots you want to print.

  3. 3 {{@icon name="mirror"}}

    Unfold

    Only the spots you rubbed print onto the other half, mirrored. Everything else stays as it was.

Works folded, too

The outer display is the back of the paper. You can't draw on it, only press.

  • Pick a small, medium or wide press, or the eraser to wipe pressed marks.
  • Undo the last press or clear everything you pressed in this fold.
  • Folders stay visible when folded. Closing the phone never opens a different sheet.

Small things that matter
